City of [[Faerin\|Faerin]]. The party's starting location and beginning of adventure. Very poor city with not many resources. The people here seem to have been abandoned and left to their own devices. City who might not survive in the coming months without food and reliable water. [[Victroria Fae\|Victroria Fae]] is Faterock's respective leader. The city does not receive any aid from the king or the other cities in the region. This town was targeted by a goblin camp and lost a lot of their people. They desecrated their graves and disrespected their dead. As of now, Faterock stands with hope in mind after the help of a few brave adventurer's.
