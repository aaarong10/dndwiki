City of [[Faerin\|Faerin]]. City with towering emerald towers and stone pillars on the outer perimeter. Emerald City is exceptionally wealthy with a huge budget for military operations. As it stands now, Emerald City is currently under the administration of its military. Ruled by [[Ozmin\|Ozmin]].
