Half elf found in [[Rothelm\|Rothelm]]. Was a mole for The Keepers who promised to give information about her family. No information she has given to [[The Keepers\|The Keepers]] has been revealed. She helped the party leave Rothelm to journey to [[Emerald City\|Emerald City]]. She ultimately met her demise at [[De la vida\|De la vida]] where she was hung from a tree and gutted.
